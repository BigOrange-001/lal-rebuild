Adaptations in GlobalData.as: only the ByteArray hashing path and rotate-right helper are included; private methods were renamed to avoid collisions, the unused public digest copy was omitted, and digest bytes are formatted directly as lowercase hexadecimal. The SHA-256 block processing and padding are unchanged. The wrapper preserves input position even on failure.
